🚀 AI Landscape Pulse – August 2026 Highlights  

📌 Google Gemini 3.7 Flash – Google just launched a lightweight, 30‑billion‑parameter “Flash” variant that cuts inference cost by 70% while keeping top‑tier reasoning performance.
