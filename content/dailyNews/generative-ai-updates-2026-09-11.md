🚀 AI Pulse: Must‑Know Breakthroughs Redefining 2026  

📌 Google Gemini 2.5 Pro + “Deep Think” – Launched June 22, Gemini 2.5 Pro adds a parallel‑reasoning mode and a 2 M‑token context window, hitting 82.4 % on GPQA‑Diamond and 89.8 % on MMLU‑Pro, now out‑performing OpenAI’s GPT‑5.5 on scientific benchmarks.  

🤖 Meta’s Muse Image Generator – Unveiled July 7, Muse is Meta’s first in‑house text‑to‑image model, ranking #2 on Arena’s leaderboard. It blends web‑search and code‑execution before rendering images, but sparks privacy debate over automatic use of public Instagram photos.  

💡 Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5 – Released June 30, the most “agentic” Claude model hits 63.2 % on coding benchmarks, offering a cost‑effective alternative to Opus 4.8 with pricing at $2 input/$10 output per M tokens.  

🧠 NVIDIA Acquires Hugging Face for $12.93 B – Announced Sept 2, the deal gives NVIDIA direct access to the leading open‑source model hub, accelerating AI‑first infrastructure and tightening the AI‑hardware‑software stack.  

🎤 NVIDIA Nemotron Speech ASR – An open‑source speech‑to‑text model 10× faster than traditional systems, now available as an NIM microservice for edge, cloud, and data‑center deployments, unlocking real‑time captions and voice‑assistant use‑cases.  

🤖 Google Gemini 3.5 Transcribe – August rollout of a new speech‑to‑text model with context‑aware transcription, low‑noise handling, and instant integration into Gemini Live, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, and Google AI Studio.
