AI Power Plays of 11/08: Funding, Partnerships, and New Infrastructure

🚀 OpenAI Secures $11 B Funding Round – In a landmark move, OpenAI closed a fresh $11 billion raise, valuing the lab at roughly $1.25 trillion and signaling continued confidence in frontier model development.

🧠 Anthropic Deepens Agent Memory Research – Anthropic publishes a paper on offline “dreaming” for agent memory, showing how idle periods can refine knowledge bases, a breakthrough for long‑term autonomous reasoning.

📈 Anthropic IPO Valuation Re‑calibrated – Post‑filing, market sentiment adjusts Anthropic’s valuation to $1.5 trillion (down from $2 trillion), reflecting the evolving dynamics of AI‑cap‑table funding.
