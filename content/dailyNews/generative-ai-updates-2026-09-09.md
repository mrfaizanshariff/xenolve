🧠 Meta launches Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber – Echoing Google, Meta’s version focuses on secure enterprise chatbots with on‑device privacy controls, aiming to win over corporations wary of cloud‑only solutions. (Meta AI blog, Aug 2026)
